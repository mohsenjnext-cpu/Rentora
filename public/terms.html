Rentora Terms of Service

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Welcome to Rentora. By accessing or using the Rentora application via the Pi Browser, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service.

1. Eligibility & Authentication

Rentora is accessible to members of the Pi Network community. Authentication must occur exclusively through the official Pi SDK. Users agree to maintain accurate profile information and comply with the Pi Network community guidelines.

2. Peer-to-Peer Rental Services

Rentora serves as a decentralized peer-to-peer directory and coordination platform connecting item owners with renters:

  • Item Owners: Must provide accurate descriptions, photos, and ensure items are safe and in working condition.
  • Renters: Must handle rented items with care and return them promptly at the end of the rental period in the same condition received.
  • Two-way Confirmation: Handover and return confirmations must be verified mutually by both parties within the application.

3. Exclusive Pi Network Currency

In strict compliance with Pi Network ecosystem policies, all transactions and platform fees on Rentora are settled exclusively in Pi cryptocurrency. No external or fiat payment methods are supported.

4. Prohibited Activities

Users are strictly prohibited from:

  • Self-renting or fabricating transactions.
  • Listing illegal, hazardous, counterfeit, or restricted items.
  • Attempting to circumvent the Pi SDK authentication or payment flow.

5. Disclaimer of Warranties

Rentora provides marketplace coordination services. Owners and renters interact directly for physical handovers. Users are encouraged to inspect items thoroughly upon physical handover.

6. Contact & Support

For questions or disputes, contact the team at developer@rentora.app or via the Pi Developer Portal.
